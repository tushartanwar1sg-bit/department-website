Welcome to the Department of Mathematics

The Department of Mathematics at Sri Venkateswara College is dedicated to developing logical thinking, analytical ability and a deep understanding of mathematical ideas. Through classroom teaching, tutorials, seminars and student-led activities, the department prepares students for higher studies, research and careers in a wide range of fields.

Explore Courses

About the Department

Sri Venkateswara College is a constituent college of the University of Delhi. The Department of Mathematics offers undergraduate courses under the University's Undergraduate Curriculum Framework and teaches Mathematics to students of many other disciplines through programme courses and generic electives.

Our Vision

To nurture curiosity, rigour and creativity in mathematics, and to develop confident students who can apply mathematical thinking to real-world problems.

Our Mission

  • Provide strong foundations in pure and applied mathematics.
  • Encourage problem solving, reasoning and independent thinking.
  • Promote the use of computational tools in mathematics.
  • Support students in higher studies, research and careers.

Courses Offered

B.Sc. (Hons.) Mathematics

A four-year undergraduate programme covering analysis, algebra, differential equations, probability, numerical methods and many electives.

Duration: 4 Years · Semesters: 8

B.A. / B.Sc. Programme

Mathematics taught as a discipline in programme courses, building strong fundamentals in calculus, algebra and their applications.

Duration: 3/4 Years

Generic Electives & SEC

Mathematics papers offered to students of other departments, along with practical Skill Enhancement Courses.

Open to all disciplines

Core Papers (B.Sc. Hons. Mathematics)

Semester Discipline Specific Core Papers
I Algebra, Elementary Real Analysis, Probability and Statistics

III Theory of Real Functions, Group Theory, Discrete Mathematics

Academic Activities

Trisectrix – The Mathematics Society

Trisectrix is the student society of the department. It organises talks, quizzes, competitions and other activities that make mathematics fun and engaging for everyone.

Seminars

The department invites speakers from academia and industry to deliver talks on topics such as:

  • Careers in Mathematics and Data Science
  • Mathematics in Cryptography
  • Famous Unsolved Problems in Mathematics

Workshops

Hands-on workshops help students learn tools that support mathematical work, such as:

  • LaTeX for Mathematical Writing
  • Python for Mathematics
  • Mathematical Software (SageMath / GeoGebra)
